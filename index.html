!

A helpful reader—not a medical decision-maker.

SafeMeds AI is currently a frontend prototype. It does not yet scan images with OCR, verify medicine authenticity, recommend doses, or determine whether a medicine is safe for you. Always check the actual package and consult a pharmacist or clinician when needed.
